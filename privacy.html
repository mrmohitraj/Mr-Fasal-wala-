🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Mr Fasal Wala ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, and how we use it to connect farmers and buyers across India.

2. Information We Collect

  • Name, mobile number and delivery address when you place an order
  • Your Google account name, email and profile photo, if you choose to log in with Google
  • Product listings and photos you upload if you sell crops or crop waste
  • Basic usage data such as pages visited, to help us improve the site

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders (Cash on Delivery)
  • To show you your order history when logged in
  • To contact you regarding your order via call or WhatsApp
  • To improve our products, pricing and services

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Your order details are shared only with our delivery team for the purpose of completing your order.

5. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no online system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

6. Your Choices

You can log out at any time. To request deletion of your account data, contact us using the details below.
